WRAP 911

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026. This policy covers the WRAP 911 trainer on the web (wrap911.com) and the WRAP 911 iOS app.

No account, no tracking

WRAP 911 has no user accounts or passwords. We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Data stored on your phone

Your license, lesson progress, drill scores and Coach chat history are saved only on your phone. Deleting the app or clearing browser data removes them.

AI Coach

The Coach can answer offline with built-in answers. It sends your question to the online AI only after you tap Allow. It then sends your question and the current chat to our server (wrap911-coach-proxy.wrap911.workers.dev, run on Cloudflare), which passes it to xAI's Grok model to write the answer. We do not ask for your name or contact details. Please do not type personal or customer information into the Coach. We do not keep chat logs on our server. xAI and Cloudflare handle the request under their own privacy policies (xAI, Cloudflare). You can withdraw permission at any time with "Reset AI permission" on the Coach screen.

Payments

Payments on wrap911.com are handled by Stripe. WRAP 911 never sees or stores your card number. Stripe may collect your email and payment details under the Stripe privacy policy.

The iOS app can also sell the Shop Pack and a Seat through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple processes that payment. WRAP 911 receives a purchase record so it can unlock the trainer on that phone and, for a Shop Pack, issue a code for the other seats. Apple's policy is at apple.com/legal/privacy.

Videos and photos

Training clips and photos load from wrap911.com. Some clips are embedded from YouTube. When one plays, YouTube may collect data under the Google privacy policy.

Children

WRAP 911 is a trade trainer for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

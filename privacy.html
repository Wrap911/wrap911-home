WRAP 911

Privacy

WRAP 911 is a shop trainer. It runs in the phone browser, or from the home screen after you add it.

The license stays on that phone. The trainer saves it in local storage so the phone stays unlocked. Clearing the browser data removes it.

Payment is handled by Stripe. WRAP 911 does not see or store your card number. Stripe’s own privacy rules cover the checkout.

The page does not run an account server. There is no password. A shop pack crew link unlocks the other phones that open it.

Clips and photos load from this site so a lesson can show the real bay.

This page is the privacy policy for the trainer at wrap911.github.io/wrap911-home.

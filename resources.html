Tools & Resources that work with Wrap 911

These are not required. They fill gaps the lesson cards do not cover: design preview, heat visualization during practice, and shop workflow. Use them alongside the trainer, not instead of it.

Design plugins (Illustrator)

WrapsDesigner Adobe Illustrator plugin
Live 3D vehicle preview next to your artboard as you draw. One-way link. Fits the "film types" and design-handoff parts of Wrap 911. wrapsdesigner.com
Vulcan plugin (VinylMaster)
Adds alignment marks and QR codes inside Illustrator so cutting data can leave AI without the full plotter software. Useful when shops hand off print/cut files. vinylmaster.software
XFCut / Easy Cut Studio plugins
Direct cut from Illustrator to common plotters (Graphtec, Roland, Mimaki, etc.). Pairs with edge and panel work in the trainer.

Visualizers (browser)

Avery Dennison free visualizer — No signup, quick color check on a vehicle. Good before ordering film that matches a Wrap 911 lesson.
Zeno (xix3D), WrapConfigurator, DreamRide.AI, WrapUp 3D, WrapsDesigner browser studio
Customer-facing previews. Use them for sales; keep Wrap 911 for the actual install technique on rivets, edges, and heat.

Training film that matches the cards

Thermochromic training vinyl (Teckwrap Redshift or similar)
Changes color near 40°C and has a printed grid. Makes heat and stretch visible during practice. Direct match for Wrap 911 heat, rivet, and edge lessons. Practice on this before customer film.

Shop software that pairs with training

Wraptor, Wrapstart, QuoteIQ, Tint Wiz, ShopVOX, GarageTool
Quoting, film inventory, job tracking. Wrap 911 covers the bay technique; these cover the business side. No integration required—just use them in parallel.

How to use with Wrap 911

  • Heat / rivets / edges cards → practice on thermochromic film so the crew can see what the card is describing.
  • Film types and color decisions → check a visualizer or WrapsDesigner before the job starts.
  • Design handoff → Illustrator plugins keep files cut-ready without leaving the design tool.
  • New hire path → run the Wrap 911 rules + sample lesson, then pair with one of the above for the shop’s actual workflow.

Wrap 911 is not affiliated with any of these tools or the film brands. Always follow the TDS for the film on the job.

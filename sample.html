Sample lesson card — van / box side panel

45–70 min 1 tech Cast film No rivet line

Goal

One clean side. Flat in raking light. Returns hold a fingernail after cool-down.

Tools

  • Surface wipe + lint-free towels
  • Felt / nylon squeegee + wrap glove
  • Heat gun on a stable base
  • Fresh blades. Magnet tray.

Steps

  1. Clean, then inspect seams, chips, and silicone. Do not wrap dirt.
  2. Hinge the panel. Set the body line first. Do not chase gloss with heat.
  3. Work the field flat. Relieve only after the field is down.
  4. Tuck returns. Post-heat edges. Walk away.
  5. Recheck after a 10-minute cool. Photo four corners and the belt line.

Fail

  • Tunnel at the character line
  • Silvering at the door gap
  • Cooked film near the slider

Pass

  • Flat in raking light
  • Return holds a fingernail
  • No lift after cool-down

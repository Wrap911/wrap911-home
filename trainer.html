Van / box — side panel, no rivet line

45–70 min1 techcast film

Goal: One clean side. No tunnels. Returns tucked.

Tools: magnets, hard card + felt, glove, heat gun, knifeless, isopropyl, tack rag.

  1. Wash, clay if needed, wipe. Panel cold and dry.
  2. Hinge the panel. Set to the body line first, not the door gap.
  3. Center out. Relieves only at relief points.
  4. Conform, then post-heat edges. Do not chase gloss with more heat.
  5. Photo four corners and the belt line before you call it done.
FAIL PHOTO SLOT — tunnel at belt line
Fail: Tunnel at the character line, silvering at the door gap, cooked film near the slider.
PASS PHOTO SLOT — flat panel, tucked returns
Pass: Flat in raking light. Returns hold a fingernail. No lift after a 10-minute cool.

Van / trailer — corrugated or rivet row

60–90 mindo not bridge rivets

Goal: Film in the valleys. Rivets seated, not tented.

  1. Work one valley at a time.
  2. Valley first, crown second, rivet last.
  3. Heat, press, cool under pressure. If it tents, pick and reseat.
  4. Overlap on a crown, never in a valley that holds water.
Fail: White rings, bridging, slit off the rivet head.
Pass: Rivet heads look painted. No air halo.

Trailer — rear doors and continuous hinge

50–80 min

Goal: Doors open and close without peeling the hinge.

  1. Align doors closed, finish edges open.
  2. Never bury the hinge barrel. Relief at every knuckle.
  3. Cut to paint, not onto moving rubber.
  4. Check latch and cam before post-heat.
Fail: Hinge bind, latch cam slices film, end-cap lift.
Pass: Full swing. End-cap return holds a slam test.

Bumper / fascia — recess and sensors

35–60 minremove if you can

Goal: Sensors clear. No thin corners.

  1. Pull the bumper if the shop allows it.
  2. Map sensors and tow hook before film goes on.
  3. Do not stretch across a recess. Cut and lay the pocket.
  4. Post-heat the corners that take parking-lot hits.
Fail: Thin corners, film over a sensor, lift in the license recess.
Pass: Even color, sensors clear, recess not bridged.

Hood / roof — large panel, heat discipline

40–75 min

Goal: No center stretch mark. No burned paint.

  1. Shade the bay. Hot panels lie.
  2. Float the sheet. Set center. Do not drag grit.
  3. Relieves only after the field is flat.
  4. Post-heat, walk away, recheck after cool-down.
Fail: Gloss change in the center, dirt comet, windshield-reveal lift.
Pass: Even gloss. Edge still down at ambient.
